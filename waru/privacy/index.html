Privacy Policy — Waru - Fitness & Routine Tracker

Effective date: 12 November 2026
Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

Waru - Fitness & Routine Tracker is an Android app operated by Renars Skietnieks, an Irish sole trader trading as Waru Studio. Waru Studio is the controller for the processing described in this notice where it determines how and why the information is used.

Contact: renar@warustudio.com
Business address: Annabel, Main Street, Dunleer, Co. Louth, Ireland

2. Information on your device

You can use local tracking without a Google sign-in. Waru stores the data you choose to enter on your device: activities, routines, workout history and statistics, profile details such as birth year, sex and height, body measurements, progress photos and app settings. Profile details, measurements and photos are optional. If you take or select a photo for progress tracking, Waru saves a copy in its local app storage.

3. Google Sign-In and cloud backups

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides an account identifier and sign-in details, such as your email address and display name. Waru uses these to identify your account and control access to account features. Signing in does not by itself turn on cloud backup.

New cloud backups require verified Pro access, sign-in and your separate, explicit agreement before the first upload. Manual and automatic backups can include activities, routines, workout history, profile information, body measurements, progress photos and supported settings. Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions provide account access, backup storage and restore. Waru records the consent version and when it was accepted. Workout data, measurements and photos in backups are used for backup and restore, not ad targeting.

To withdraw cloud-backup consent, choose Sign Out in Settings → Backup & Restore. Uploads pause on that device immediately. If offline, Waru retries the withdrawal when connected; you may need to sign in again to finish it. Withdrawal does not delete existing backups or disable local tracking.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

The free version can show Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process device and app information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, advertising or app identifiers, and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Waru checks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent status before requesting ads. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry, you can review or change your choices in Settings → About → Ad privacy options. Advertising choices are separate from cloud-backup consent. Pro removes in-app ads.

Waru does not integrate the Firebase Analytics SDK or send Firebase Analytics events. Google services, Android and Google Play can still process data for their own advertising, payments, security or diagnostic functions. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Google Play purchases

Google Play processes payments. Waru does not receive your card or bank details. Waru uses product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase or subscription status to verify Pro access. If you verify a purchase without Google Sign-In, Firebase creates an anonymous account identifier for billing verification. The verification service keeps purchase and entitlement records linked to the Firebase identifier and can link verified access to your Waru account if you sign in later. Google handles its own payment records separately.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, we process information to provide requested account services and paid features under our contract with you, and rely on your separate consent to upload and store your optional backups. Where backup contents reveal health information, we rely on your explicit consent for that processing. We rely on consent for advertising processing where required. We use legitimate interests to secure the service, prevent misuse and respond to support requests where those interests are not overridden by your rights. We retain records required by law to meet applicable legal obligations. You can withdraw consent without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

7. Service providers and international processing

We use Google Firebase for account and cloud services, Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases and our email provider for support. Waru does not sell your workouts, measurements or photos. Our backup database and functions are in Europe and our backup storage is in the United States. Other Google services may also process data internationally. See Firebase privacy and security and Google Cloud's Data Processing Addendum for Google's processing terms and transfer safeguards. You can contact us for information about safeguards applicable to your data.

8. Retention and Pro expiry

Local records remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app's data. Waru Studio cannot remotely erase local copies. Cloud backup keeps up to two completed snapshots, subject to storage limits. Removing a local entry does not immediately erase it from an older snapshot. Pro expiry stops new uploads, but does not by itself delete your existing backups. You can sign in to restore an available backup or request deletion without active Pro.

We keep account and purchase-verification records as needed to operate accounts, verify purchases, restore paid access and handle billing disputes. Records tied to a separate anonymous Firebase identifier, or purchase records without an assigned owner, are not automatically attributable to a deleted Google-linked Waru account. We keep records needed to verify active subscriptions or lifetime purchases. Contact us if you want to request deletion of billing data that you believe relates to you. Support records are kept as needed to respond to requests or resolve disputes; records required by law are kept for the applicable period. Google retains its own advertising and payment data under its policies.

9. Request Waru account and cloud-data deletion

While signed in, open Settings → Backup & Restore and choose Delete Account & Cloud Data. Confirm the request and sign in again if asked. To request deletion without the app, use the Waru account deletion page.

Account deletion removes your Waru authentication account, associated cloud backups and photos, and purchase-verification and entitlement records owned by that Firebase account. Accepted in-app requests stop new uploads and wait at least 30 minutes before scheduled cleanup; failed work is retried. Deleted storage objects can remain in the provider's recovery layer for seven days, unavailable through Waru. A minimal deletion receipt containing the account identifier and job timestamps is retained for seven days after successful cleanup. Account deletion does not remove local records, delete your Google account or Google's payment records, or cancel a Google Play subscription.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Contact renar@warustudio.com about data Waru Studio controls. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission or your local supervisory authority.

11. Security, age and changes

Waru uses authenticated account checks and HTTPS for cloud requests. Local data also depends on your device's security. Waru is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We will update this notice as the app or applicable requirements change and give additional notice where required.
