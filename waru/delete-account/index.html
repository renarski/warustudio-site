Delete your Waru account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Use this page to request deletion of your Waru - Fitness & Routine Tracker account and associated cloud data. You do not need active Pro.

Request deletion by email

Email a deletion request to renar@warustudio.com.

Say that you want your Waru account and cloud data deleted. If possible, write from the address you use to sign in with Google. If you cannot access that address or never signed in with Google, tell us so we can help verify your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, workouts or photos.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Waru and open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. Choose Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm. If prompted, sign in with Google again and confirm the request.

Once accepted, an in-app request stops new uploads. Cleanup starts after a safety delay of at least 30 minutes. Failed cleanup is retried, so accepting the request does not mean deletion is complete.

What is deleted and what may remain

Deletion removes your Waru authentication account, cloud backups and their contents (including progress photos), and billing verification and entitlement records owned by that Firebase account. It does not delete your Google account or Waru data stored on your devices.

Deleted cloud objects can remain in the storage provider's recovery layer for seven days and are unavailable through Waru during that time. A minimal deletion receipt is kept for seven days after successful cleanup. Separate anonymous or unassigned billing records may remain if they cannot be reliably linked to your account or are needed to verify paid access. Google keeps its own payment records separately. See the Waru Privacy Policy for details.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting a Waru account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop renewal, use Google Play subscriptions. You do not have to cancel before requesting deletion.
