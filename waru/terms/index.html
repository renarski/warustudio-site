6. Cloud backup, expiry and deletion

New backups require an internet connection, Google Sign-In, verified Pro and your backup consent. Waru supports manual and automatic backups and keeps up to two completed versions, subject to storage limits. Backup and restore may be delayed or unavailable, and restoring a snapshot can replace current local records. Check that a backup has completed before relying on it and use the local export feature to keep a separate copy of information that matters to you.

When Pro expires, free features remain available and new uploads stop. Expiry does not itself delete existing backups; sign in to restore an available backup or request deletion. Signing out withdraws backup consent without deleting existing copies.
